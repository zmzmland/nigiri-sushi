にぎり寿司Nigiri Sushi

How to play
1

Put the sushi on the board

Lay out what the customer ordered — one piece at a time, kept apart.

2

Take your hand away

There is no button. The moment your hand is clear, it counts down 3–2–1.

3

Here you are!

Changed your mind? Touch a piece while it counts and the order resets.

Getting started

  • Place the TUNA to open the shop
  • Place the SALMON for Japanese
  • Place the SHRIMP for English

Good to know

  • Three customers, about two minutes in all
  • The faster you serve, the more you earn
  • The third customer gets no order slip — you'll have to remember it
  • Please clear the board when you're done
